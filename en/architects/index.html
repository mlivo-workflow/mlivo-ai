1 · AI in CAD

AI right inside your CAD software — via MCP connector

Using the Model Context Protocol (MCP), I connect AI assistants such as Claude directly to your design tools. You describe what you need in plain words — the AI creates the 3D model in FreeCAD and changes it on request.

🏠
Variants & massing studies in minutes

Building volumes, roof shapes, garage left or right — several variants for the first client meeting, without hours of modelling.

🔁
Hand off routine work

Placing windows, laying out walls on a grid, naming and organising parts — the assistant takes over the repetitive steps.

📐
Ask your model

“What is the footprint?” or “List all windows with dimensions” — answers straight from the model instead of measuring by hand.

🛠️
Set up in your office

I install and configure the connector on your workstations, train your team and, on request, build custom tools for your standards.

From a real project
To be clear: the AI delivers drafts and groundwork — design, checking and responsibility stay with you. Today this runs with FreeCAD; whether your software can be connected (e.g. via an API) is something I check in the intro call. Your CAD files stay on your computer; your instructions and the model information needed for them are sent to the AI service.
2 · 4D construction schedule

See the construction sequence before it is built

4D means: 3D model plus construction schedule. Every component is linked to its date — for any given day the visualisation shows what the site should look like according to plan. Your drawings and schedule become a video, an image sequence or an interactive page.

🔍
Check the sequence before it gets expensive

Sequencing errors are obvious in the picture — scaffolding removed too early, or a crane that is still needed.

👥
Everyone sees the same sequence

Clients, authorities and trades understand the schedule without a Gantt chart — ideal for meetings and presentations.

🏗️
Site logistics made visible

Crane, containers, scaffolding and access routes appear and disappear at the right time in the model.

🔁
Schedule change? New images.

If a date moves, the visualisation is regenerated from the schedule — no redrawing.

In short: 4D in construction in 60 seconds (YouTube, German)

To be clear: the 4D visualisation shows your schedule — it does not replace scheduling. Interior work such as installation, screed or fit-out is not visible from outside, and the level of detail depends on the model. Whether your drawings and schedule (e.g. from MS Project or Excel) can be used directly is something I check in the intro call.
