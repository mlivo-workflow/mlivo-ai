FAQ

Answers before you have to ask.

What does it cost to get started?

The first conversation is free. After that: AI talk € 590, workshops from € 1,590, AI assessment € 2,990. All prices are final amounts without VAT; travel within Upper Austria is included.

Do I have to buy an AI agent right away?

No. Many start with a workshop or the assessment. Whether anything gets built afterwards — and by whom — is your decision. I don't sell software licences.

Do we need technical knowledge?

No. For AI agents I handle setup and operation; you get a short introduction. If you'd rather implement yourself, I guide you with AI sparring.

How long until an AI agent is running?

Starter: approx. 4–6 weeks. Business: step by step in approx. 2–3 months. The start date is agreed bindingly in advance.

What happens to our data (GDPR)?

The automation runs in the EU by default. You choose the AI model: OpenAI with EU data residency where available, a European provider, or fully local on your own hardware. A data-processing agreement and TOMs are included.

What if we get more inquiries than included?

Each additional 1,000 inquiries started cost € 18. If your volume stays higher, we adjust the monthly fee together — in advance, never by surprise.

Does the training count towards the AI-literacy duty (Art. 4 EU AI Act)?

Every workshop includes handouts and a certificate of participation, which you can use to document the AI-literacy measure for your team.

Are we tied to you?

AI agents: minimum term 6 months from go-live, then cancellable monthly. AI sparring: minimum 3 months. When you leave, you receive an export of your workflows and data.

Do you also work outside Upper Austria?

Workshops, sparring and AI agents also work online. On site I'm mainly in the Linz, Linz-Land and Enns region; outside Upper Austria travel is charged at cost. On-premise only in Upper Austria.
