Who writes the minutes? Mlivo Protokoll drafts them.

How it works

From recording to sent minutes in four steps

🎙️
1 · Record

With a phone, dictation device or conference microphone. Before uploading, you confirm that everyone present has agreed — this is logged.

🖥️
2 · Transcribe in your office

A device in your office converts the recording in the background — if you like, only at night. Transcript with timestamps, searchable.

📝
3 · Draft minutes

Turn passages of the transcript into decisions, agreements, actions with owner and due date, or information. Optionally Claude or ChatGPT can suggest the draft.

✅
4 · Review and send

You approve. PDF with your letterhead, open items move into the next meeting automatically. Sent versions stay unchanged.

Your data

Stays in your office

A ready-to-use device in your office (e.g. Mac Mini), encrypted storage, backup. Recordings are deleted automatically after a period you set.

GDPR

Consent, retention, erasure

Consent is mandatory before every upload. Access requests and erasure are built in. Data processing agreement for remote maintenance included.

People decide

Only you can send

AI writes drafts at most. Approving, sending and deleting stay with you.

To be clear: Mlivo Protokoll is new and is being introduced together with pilot businesses. Speech recognition is not error-free — a person always reviews the minutes. It does not yet tell speakers apart; you assign names in the draft. How fast a recording is converted depends on the device. Recordings only with the consent of everyone present. If you use Claude or ChatGPT for the draft, you need your own business account, and only the transcript is sent to that provider.
