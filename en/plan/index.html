Mlivo Plan in 60 seconds · no sound · demo data

1 · Drawing issue

Proof instead of searching through email folders

Drawings, revisions and approvals in one place. Transmittals get a gap-free number, a PDF with your letterhead and a ZIP of all drawings. The revision matrix shows at a glance which recipient still has an old revision.

📑
Transmittal in two minutes

Pick recipients, the latest approved revisions are pre-selected. Number, PDF and ZIP are created automatically.

✅
Four-eyes approval

Only approved revisions can be issued. Who approved what and when is logged.

📘
Drawing register as PDF or Excel

The complete history per project — for the client, the site or a dispute.

📥
Bring your existing list

Import your current drawing list from Excel (CSV) with a preview before anything is saved.

2 · Meetings & tasks

Minutes that carry themselves forward

Open items move into the next meeting automatically — with their number and origin. Once sent, minutes are frozen; corrections only as a new version with a note. One task list across all projects.

🔁
Nothing gets lost

Whatever is open moves on. What was completed last time appears once more as done — then it disappears.

🎙️
Recording to text, locally

With everyone's consent, record the meeting and have it transcribed overnight on your own server — the recording never leaves your server. Each passage can become an item.

📄
Minutes as PDF

Grouped by decisions, agreements, actions and information, with attendees, distribution list and objection notice. German or English.

⏰
Overdue at a glance

Task list across all projects, filtered by company, person or due date — and as CSV for the Monday meeting.

3 · Overview, AI, data

Small, safe, yours

Claude & ChatGPT

AI with guard rails

Ask in plain language: “Who still has revision B of the ground-floor plan?” or “Turn the transcript into draft minutes.” The AI reads and drafts — issuing, sending and deleting stay with people.

Your data

EU server or in your office

A dedicated server per office in the EU — or a computer in your office (Windows, Mac, Linux). Encrypted, daily backup, full export at any time, GDPR functions built in.

Deliberately lean

Set up in half a day

No invoices, fees or time tracking — only the modules you need. It runs alongside your existing software.

To be clear: Mlivo Plan is new and is being introduced together with pilot offices. It sends no emails itself — PDF and ZIP are passed on as before. The AI connection needs your own business account with Claude or ChatGPT. Recordings only with the consent of everyone present.
